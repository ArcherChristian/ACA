Admissions

A Covenant Partnership between the school, the student, and the family.

More Than a Checklist

Admissions is not a checklist to complete, but a shared journey of discernment.

Archer Christian Academy is built on a covenant relationship between the school, the student, and the family. We partner with families who desire a Christ-centered, classical education that shapes children to renew Englewood and glorify God. We are looking for families who share our mission and are ready to walk this path with us.

The Covenant Process

1

Conversation & Tour

Every partnership begins with a conversation. We invite families to visit, see our classrooms in action, and share their hopes for their children.

2

Application & Family Story

Families complete an online application—more than forms, it’s an opportunity to tell your story, your faith journey, and your educational values.

3

Partnership Interview

Each family meets personally with our leadership team to discuss our mission, Statement of Faith, and educational philosophy. Together, we prayerfully discern if Archer is the right community for your child.

4

Enrollment Decision

Our team considers each application with care and prayer. When families are accepted, we celebrate the beginning of a shared covenant—joining hands to form and launch students true.

A Shared Investment

A core part of our mission is to make this transformative education accessible to the families of Englewood. Our tuition model is built on a partnership of shared investment, stewardship, and community support.

The true cost to educate a student at Archer Christian Academy is significant, but it is almost entirely covered by the generous support of ministry partners who believe in our mission. This allows us to offer a deeply subsidized rate to our families.

We understand that this commitment represents a significant sacrifice for many families. It is an investment of trust in our shared mission. Should this amount present a challenge, we are committed to working directly and confidentially with every accepted family to create a manageable plan.

Our goal is to ensure that no mission-aligned family is prevented from joining our community for financial reasons.
