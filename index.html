Academics

A Classical Foundation

We do not launch arrows by chance, but by purpose. An Archer education is:

  • Unapologetically Christian A biblical lens for all learning—faith woven through every subject.
  • Intentionally Classical The Trivium forges ordered, articulate, and discerning minds.
  • Culturally Conservative We steward and transmit a rich, stabilizing heritage.

Opening with K–2 in Fall 2027—then adding one grade every year, all the way to 12th.

Tuition

An Education Within Reach

The true cost of an Archer education is almost entirely covered by ministry partners who believe in this mission. Every family contributes monthly—an amount set together, shaped by what your family is able to do. Cost will never decide whether a child belongs here.

“No mission-aligned family will be prevented from joining our community for financial reasons.”
