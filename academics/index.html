Culturally Conservative

Pedagogical Method

At Archer Academy, instruction is Christ-centered, with both our curriculum and our culture ordered toward God’s glory. Our teaching follows the classical Trivium, aligning learning with each child’s natural stage of development.

Christ-Centered Instruction: The curriculum and culture are ordered to God’s glory.

The Trivium: We teach students how to think by matching instruction to their developmental stage—Grammar (K–5), Logic (6–8), and Rhetoric (9–12).

Practical & Tangible Learning: We integrate hands-on skills like woodworking and archery, grounding knowledge in real-world skill.

Intentionally Communal: We are a small community by design, where every student is known and shaped with care.

The Academic Program: Bow in Hand

Our educational model is shaped by the time-tested legacy of classical learning. Through the Trivium and hands-on disciplines, we train students to think clearly, work skillfully, and grow in virtue.

The Grammar Stage (K–5th Grade)

Enrollment Open for K–2

In these formative years, the mind is open and eager. We fill it with wonder and truth through joyful discovery, recitation, and memorization.

Core Subjects:
  • Phonics-based reading
  • Classic children’s literature
  • Chronological history
  • Mathematics focused on mastery
  • Observational science to cultivate wonder
Enrichment:
  • Latin opens language and the legacy of the West
  • Scripture, music, and art shape the heart toward what is good, true, and beautiful
Applied Arts:
  • Archery and woodworking teach discipline, safety, and the quiet focus required to shape both tools and character

The Logic Stage (6th–8th Grade)

As students begin to question and reason, we equip them with the tools to think clearly and discern truth. Instruction moves from what to why, training minds to see order and meaning.

Core Subjects:
  • Integrated studies in history, literature, Latin, formal logic, mathematics, and the sciences
  • Explored through Socratic discussion
Developing Skills:
  • Students refine analysis, debate, and written argument
  • Connecting the logic of design to the precision of craftsmanship

The Rhetoric Stage (9th–12th Grade)

In the final stage, knowledge matures into wisdom. Students learn to synthesize ideas and communicate with clarity, conviction, and grace.

Core Subjects:
  • The Great Books anchor the humanities
  • Joined by advanced mathematics, science, theology, and government
Capstone Experience:
  • Each senior researches, writes, and publicly defends a thesis
  • While also crafting a masterful piece of woodworking—a union of intellect, faith, and skill that launches them beyond the academy

Spiritual Life & Discipleship: The Archer’s Aim

Education at Archer is, above all, an act of discipleship. Our aim is to orient every student’s heart, mind, and hands toward Christ. Faith is woven through every subject, conversation, and daily rhythm of life in our community.

Daily Rhythms

Each day begins with prayer and Scripture, and every class is taught from the conviction that all truth is God’s truth (Colossians 1:16-17).

Integrated Faith

In science, we marvel at a Creator’s design. In history, we trace God’s redemptive plan. In literature, we explore the human condition and need for a Savior.

Weekly Chapel

The entire school community gathers weekly for worship, prayer, and teaching from God’s Word, reinforcing our shared identity as a family in Christ.

Mentorship

Our teachers do more than instruct; they mentor. They model a life of faith, intentionally investing in students to guide their spiritual and personal formation.
