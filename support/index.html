The Opportunity Before Us

Englewood is one of Chicago’s most under-resourced communities—and one of its most resilient. Families here are hungry for real options. Archer fills that gap with a proven model that has transformed communities across the country.

Why Englewood. Why Now.

For four decades, Urban Youth Outreach has served families in this community. We know these families, we know this neighborhood, and we know what it takes to build something that lasts.

Archer is not a reaction to what is broken—it is a proactive answer, born from everything we have learned. This is a first-mover opportunity to plant a school where the impact per dollar is among the highest anywhere in the nation.

See the need in numbers →

A Proven Network Behind Us

Archer is modeled on Hope Academy in Minneapolis and guided through its launch by the Spreading Hope Network—which has launched and sustained Christ-centered schools in underserved urban communities for over 20 years—while partnering with Clapham School on curriculum and teacher training.

Field-tested. Mentored. Built on a track record—not theory.

Bridging the Gap

The true cost of an Archer education is about $12,000 per student each year. Families contribute roughly ten percent, as they are able—because cost should never decide who belongs—and founding partners bridge the rest.

Founding Partnerships

An Invitation to Build

Every child in Englewood carries the image of God and the potential for greatness. What they are missing is not ability—it is aim. When you join us as a founding partner, you are not sponsoring a project—you are sponsoring a person. And person by person, we launch a generation.

Student Sponsor

$10,800 or $900 a month

The Arrow is the student. Fully funds one student’s tuition for the academic year—the gap between what a family can give and the true cost.

Semester Partner

$5,400 or $450 a month

Provides complete support for one student for a semester.

Fletching Partner

$3,600 or $300 a month

An arrow needs three. You join two other partners to fully fund a student.

Archer Partner

$25,000 a launch gift

The Archer is the school itself. Helps establish the academy—our home, our team, our launch.

Advocate ($1,200 or $100 a month) and Supporter ($600 or $50 a month) gifts underwrite program materials and the ongoing foundation of the school. Every level can be given once or monthly at archerchristian.org/partner.

Built-In Accountability

Our budget is lean and grounded in real-world data from comparable schools. Network oversight from the Spreading Hope Network and transparent financial reporting ensure every dollar is invested wisely.
