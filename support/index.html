The Opportunity Before Us

Englewood is one of Chicago’s most under-resourced communities—and one of its most resilient. Families here are hungry for real options. The public school system has struggled for decades, and private alternatives are virtually nonexistent. Archer Christian Academy fills that gap with a proven educational model that has transformed communities across the country.

True community renewal requires both spiritual regeneration and intellectual formation. We are passionate about launching students who are not only employable but are virtuous, kingdom-minded leaders.

Our Engine

Demonstrable life transformation.

Our engine runs on trust. Because partners see the fruit—students growing in character and knowledge—they supply the resources that allow us to serve more families.

Why Englewood. Why Now.

For over three decades, Englewood Family Outreach has served families in this community. That work has given us a clarity of vision that only comes from years on the ground. We know these families. We know this neighborhood. And we know what it takes to build something that lasts.

Archer is not a reaction to what is broken—it is a proactive answer, born from everything we have learned. This is a first-mover opportunity to plant a school where the impact per dollar is among the highest anywhere in the nation.

A Proven Network Behind Us

Archer Christian Academy is not starting from scratch. We are partnered with the Spreading Hope Network, a national organization that has helped launch and sustain Christ-centered schools in underserved urban communities for over 20 years.

We are also partnering with Clapham School for curriculum development and teacher training. This means our model is field-tested, our operations are guided by experienced mentors, and our plan is built on a track record of success—not theory.

Bridging the Gap

To make this education accessible, we ask families to contribute $100/month. However, the actual cost of a high-quality classical education is significantly higher. We need partners to bridge that gap.
