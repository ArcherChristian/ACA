The Six Strands — CC Challenge

Every school day, all six, 45–60 minutes each (Ari, Ti, Rissa). Content deepens by Challenge level, but the six strands stay the same A → IV.

StrandWhat it is
1 · LogicMathematics — daily math work.
2 · GrammarLatin — vocabulary, grammar, translation.
3 · ResearchScience — biology/astronomy in Challenge A; research + lab skills up the levels.
4 · ExpositionLiterature + composition — reading and persuasive/essay writing.
5 · DebateGeography/cartography in Challenge A; history, government + current events at higher levels.
6 · ReasoningLogic, analogies + fallacies; philosophy and apologetics at higher levels.

Bo does Foundations-level work instead: reading, Bible copywork + journal, TMM math, and CC memory work — 1-on-1 with Mom. Friday community day = all six strands with tutors, so home strands run Mon–Thu.
