Our Guiding Metaphor

Our entire methodology is contained within the relationship between the Archer and the Arrow.

The Academy

The Archer

The school itself—choosing the targets, timing, and stance, activating the bow with discipline and a servant’s heart.

The Educational Model

The Bow

Hewn from a classical legacy, shaped like an open book—storing the potential energy and setting the line of flight.

The Student

The Arrow

Prepared, aimed, and launched with care—polished for God’s purposes and sent out to bring renewal to Englewood.

The Teachers

The String

Mentors who bear the tension of high standards and transfer the bow’s energy to the arrow with care and precision.

The Portrait of a Graduate

Every graduate is an arrow launched not for self-glory, but to bring renewal. Shaped and sharpened for lasting impact.

1

Shaft

Forged in discipline and truth, growing straight and strong, free from the bends of compromise.

2

Fletching

Guided by the Good, the True, and the Beautiful, which keep their flight stable and true.

3

Point

Sharpened by virtue—wisdom, courage, and justice—able to make a lasting impact without shattering.

An Archer graduate will be a young man or woman who thinks critically, communicates persuasively, creates with purpose, loves learning, discerns truth, embodies virtue, and is prepared for a life of service.

A Classical Foundation

We do not launch arrows by chance, but by purpose. Our educational model is:

  • Unapologetically Christian A biblical lens for all learning. Faith is woven through every subject.
  • Intentionally Classical The Trivium forges ordered, articulate, and discerning minds.
  • Culturally Conservative We steward and transmit a rich, stabilizing heritage.

Spiritual Life & Discipleship

Education at Archer is, above all, an act of discipleship. Faith is woven through every subject, conversation, and daily rhythm.

Daily Rhythms

Each day begins with prayer and Scripture, and every class is taught from the conviction that all truth is God’s truth.

Integrated Faith

In science, we marvel at a Creator’s design. In history, we trace God’s redemptive plan. In literature, we explore the human condition and need for a Savior.

Weekly Chapel

The entire school community gathers weekly for worship, prayer, and teaching from God’s Word, reinforcing our shared identity as a family in Christ.

Mentorship

Our teachers do more than instruct; they mentor. They model a life of faith, intentionally investing in students to guide their spiritual formation.
