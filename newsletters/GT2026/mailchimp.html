Happy Thanksgiving from Englewood

A New Thing

A thank-you note, and an invitation to help start something.

 

Thanksgiving dinner at the after-school club, Marquette Road, Englewood.

*|IF:FNAME|*Dear *|FNAME|*,*|ELSE:|*Dear Friends,*|END:IF|*

Happy Thanksgiving! Last Tuesday the after-school club had its Thanksgiving dinner a week early: turkey and dressing, cornbread muffins, a bowl of apples, and two folding tables full of kids who had been in school all day and came anyway (several of them for seconds). We have been setting that table (well, maybe not that exact table!) for forty years, and it has not gotten old.

“Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise! Give thanks to him; bless his name! For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.”

Psalm 100.4-5

So this is mostly a thank-you note. As we shared earlier this month, $250,000 of the $400,000 needed to open Archer Christian Academy is committed, and the pledges from the breakfast are still trickling in. Every dollar of that came from someone who believed in a school that does not exist yet… it is marvelous to behold the generosity of God’s people. Thank you!
$250,000 committed of the $400,000 needed to open
 
   

$150,000 to go. Our aim is to have it committed by year’s end.

Apples in the yard, and the line for turkey.

Isaiah 43.19

“Behold, I am doing a new thing; now it springs forth, do you not perceive it? I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert.”

That leaves $150,000, and we know it is not a small number. It is the part that turns a plan into a school: three founding teachers (found, hired, and trained), a building in Englewood (found and made ready), books, desks, curriculum, and all the things nobody thinks about until the first day of school arrives and they are not there.

When Nehemiah told the people what God had put in his heart to do, “they said, ‘Let us rise up and build.’ So they strengthened their hands for the good work” (Nehemiah 2.18). That is the invitation we are making. We are not asking you to keep something going; we are asking you to help start something.

If you give there on December 1 (you will find us listed as Urban Youth Outreach, the nonprofit behind Archer), a $250 gift becomes $500. We would love to claim the whole $10,000, which takes forty of you at $250 each, early in the day. (Terms apply, obviously; PayPal’s are at the link.)

Two folding tables, and forty years of Thanksgivings.

Also, two small things. If you have already given, thank you, and would you forward this to one friend who has not heard of Archer yet? And whether or not you give, pray with us. God “is able to make all grace abound” (2 Corinthians 9.8), and we are asking Him for the rest of the money and for a school home in Englewood to go with it.
