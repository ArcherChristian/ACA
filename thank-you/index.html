What Your Partnership Means

Archer Christian Academy will open its doors in August 2027 to kindergarten through second grade, and every one of those seats will be held open by partners like you. The true cost of educating a student is about $12,000 a year, and families will carry roughly a tenth of that. The rest is carried by people who believe that the children of Englewood deserve an education as rigorous, as beautiful, and as rooted in Christ as any in the city. Today you became one of them.

“Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above” (Jas. 1.17). We receive yours as exactly that, and we will steward it with care.

Your receipt is on its way. A confirmation email with your tax-deductible receipt is being sent to the address you provided. Gifts are made to Urban Youth Outreach, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 36-3382527), the parent ministry of Archer Christian Academy, and are designated to the school.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, please check your spam folder, then contact us and we will send it again.

About your monthly gift. Your card will be charged on this date each month, and you will receive a receipt by email each time. A year-end summary of all your gifts will follow in January.

To edit, pause, or change your donation at any time, use our update form or email daniel@archerchristian.org. No form is required if you simply let us know what you would like changed.
