Founding Teachers • Fall 2027

Do What Few Educators Ever Get To Do

Archer opens in Fall 2027 with three Grammar classrooms—Kindergarten, 1st, and 2nd grade—and adds a grade every year through 12th. Founding teachers won’t inherit a culture; they will set it. The traditions you start, the standards you hold, and the children you love in year one will shape this school for a generation.

We are building relationships with prospective teachers now, well ahead of launch. If the vision below sounds like you, we would love to start the conversation early.

Formation Over Information

Christ-centered, classical teaching that shapes mind, body, and soul—wonder and truth, not test-prep.

Small By Design

14 students per class. You will truly know every child in your room—and be known by them.

Trained & Supported

Classical pedagogy training and coaching through our curriculum partnership with Clapham School. No prior classical experience required—we grow our teachers.

Who We’re Looking For

  • Teachers who love Christ and love children, and embrace our Statement of Faith
  • Educators hungry to master their craft in the classical tradition—we train, you grow
  • People who believe Englewood’s children deserve the best education in the city
  • Builders—founding teachers help shape a school, not just fill a role

Formal openings will be posted as we approach launch—but early conversations are welcome any time.
