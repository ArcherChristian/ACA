Application for Fall 2027

We are currently accepting pre-enrollment applications for children entering Kindergarten, 1st Grade, or 2nd Grade in the Fall of 2027.

The Process

  1. Submit this Form: Tell us about your family and students.
  2. Family Interview: We will contact you to schedule a partnership meeting.
  3. Assessment: A brief readiness assessment for your student.
  4. Enrollment: Sign the family partnership covenant and confirm your tuition plan.

Note: Completing this form does not guarantee enrollment, but starts the formal conversation. Tuition is never a barrier—each family’s contribution is shaped by their means, together and confidentially.

Family Information

Include student name(s), age(s), grade(s) for Fall 2027, home church (if any), and any questions. Example: Titus (Age 5, Kindergarten), Faith (Age 7, 2nd Grade).
