The Academic Program

The Trivium, Stage by Stage

Learning aligned with each child’s natural stage of growth—training students to think clearly, work skillfully, and grow in virtue.

K – 5th Grade

The Grammar Stage

Enrolling K–2 for Fall 2027

In these formative years, the mind is open and eager. We fill it with wonder and truth through joyful discovery, recitation, and memorization.

What Grammar students learn
Core Subjects
  • Phonics-based reading
  • Classic children’s literature
  • Chronological history
  • Mathematics focused on mastery
  • Observational science to cultivate wonder
Enrichment
  • Latin opens language and the legacy of the West
  • Scripture, music, and art shape the heart toward the good, true, and beautiful
Applied Arts
  • Archery and woodworking teach discipline, safety, and quiet focus
6th – 8th Grade

The Logic Stage

As students begin to question and reason, we equip them to think clearly and discern truth. Instruction moves from what to why.

What Logic students learn
Core Subjects
  • Integrated history, literature, Latin, formal logic, mathematics, and sciences
  • Explored through Socratic discussion
Developing Skills
  • Analysis, debate, and written argument
  • Connecting the logic of design to the precision of craftsmanship
9th – 12th Grade

The Rhetoric Stage

Knowledge matures into wisdom. Students learn to synthesize ideas and communicate with clarity, conviction, and grace.

What Rhetoric students learn
Core Subjects
  • The Great Books anchor the humanities
  • Advanced mathematics, science, theology, and government
Capstone Experience
  • Each senior researches, writes, and publicly defends a thesis
  • While crafting a masterful piece of woodworking—intellect, faith, and skill united
The Archer’s Aim

Spiritual Life & Discipleship

Education at Archer is, above all, an act of discipleship—orienting every student’s heart, mind, and hands toward Christ.

Daily Rhythms

Each day begins with prayer and Scripture, and every class is taught from the conviction that all truth is God’s truth (Colossians 1:16–17).

Integrated Faith

In science, we marvel at a Creator’s design. In history, we trace God’s redemptive plan. In literature, we explore the human condition and need for a Savior.

Weekly Chapel

The entire school community gathers weekly for worship, prayer, and teaching from God’s Word—one family in Christ.

Mentorship

Our teachers do more than instruct; they mentor—modeling a life of faith and intentionally investing in each student’s formation.
