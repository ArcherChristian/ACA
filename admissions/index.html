Tuition & Financial Commitment

A Shared Investment

A core part of our mission is to make this transformative education accessible to the families of Englewood. The true cost of an Archer education is significant—and it is almost entirely covered by ministry partners who believe in our mission.

Every family contributes monthly toward tuition. The amount is not a posted price: it is determined individually, based on each family’s financial resources, and settled together—directly and confidentially—during enrollment.

Our goal is simple: no mission-aligned family will be prevented from joining our community for financial reasons.

How Family Tuition Works

Three commitments
  • Every family invests. A monthly contribution keeps every family a vested partner in their children’s education.
  • Set by your means. The amount is based on your family’s financial resources—never a one-size-fits-all price.
  • Decided together. After acceptance, we sit down with you—confidentially—to shape a plan that works.
Financial circumstances will not be a barrier to enrollment.
