Your Gift at Work

Archer Christian Academy is building something rare—a school where rigorous academics, deep character formation, and the Gospel meet in one of Chicago’s most under-resourced communities. The true cost of an Archer education is about $12,000 per student each year. Families contribute roughly ten percent of that, as they are able, and partners bridge the rest.

Partnership Levels

Each level can be given at once or spread across the year as a monthly gift.

  • Archer Partner$25,000The Archer is the school itself—a foundational gift toward the launch of the academy.
  • Student Sponsor$10,800 or $900/mo.Fully funds one student’s tuition for the academic year.
  • Semester Partner$5,400 or $450/mo.Provides complete support for one student for a semester.
  • Fletching Partner$3,600 or $300/mo.An arrow needs three—you join two other partners to fully fund a student.
  • Advocate$1,200 or $100/mo.Underwrites essential program materials and operational launch costs.
  • Supporter$600 or $50/mo.Provides vital ongoing support to build the foundation of our new program.

The Archer Cornerstone Fund

Every great institution is built on a strong foundation. The Cornerstone Fund is our campaign to lay the groundwork for Archer Christian Academy—not for operations, but for establishment. We estimate that about $400,000 will carry Archer through its launch: getting the word out to families, purchasing curriculum, furnishing classrooms, hiring teachers, and everything else it takes to open our doors on August 23, 2027. Gifts serve three mission-critical goals:

  • Secure Our Founding Team. Bring on the key personnel and strategic partners required for a successful launch.
  • Establish Our Home. Secure and prepare our physical location in Englewood.
  • Build Our Plan. Cover the administrative, legal, and strategic consulting costs needed to build an excellent, sustainable school from day one.

A Cornerstone gift is a first-in, foundational investment. You are not just supporting a school—you are one of its builders.

Questions Partners Ask

Giving to Archer, Answered

Is my gift to Archer Christian Academy tax-deductible?

Yes. Archer Christian Academy is a ministry of Urban Youth Outreach, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 36-3382527), so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You receive a receipt for every gift.

What does it cost to educate one student at Archer?

About $12,000 per student each year. Families contribute roughly ten percent of that, as they are able, and partners cover the rest. A Student Sponsor gift of $10,800 (or $900 a month) carries one child for a full year; a Semester Partner gift of $5,400 ($450 a month) carries a child for a semester; and three Fletching Partners at $3,600 ($300 a month) together fund one student.

Can I give to Archer monthly?

Yes. Choose Monthly on the form above and give $900, $450, $300, $100, $50, or any amount each month. You can change the amount, the payment method, or the charge date—or pause the gift—on the update page.

Can I give through a donor-advised fund, by check, with stock, or from an IRA?

Yes. Donor-advised fund grants (including National Christian Foundation), checks, IRA qualified charitable distributions, stock gifts, multi-year pledges, and employer matching gifts are all welcome—see More Ways to Give. Checks are payable to Archer Christian Academy and mailed to 1507 E 53rd St, Box 691, Chicago, IL 60615; donor-advised fund grants, IRA distributions, and matching gifts are directed to Urban Youth Outreach (EIN 36-3382527), designated “Archer Christian Academy.”

What do gifts toward launch costs pay for?

We estimate that about $400,000 will carry Archer through its launch: getting the word out to families, purchasing curriculum, furnishing classrooms, hiring teachers, and everything else needed to open our doors on August 23, 2027.
