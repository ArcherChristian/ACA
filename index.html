Questions People Ask

Archer, Answered

What is Archer Christian Academy?

Archer Christian Academy is a private, Christ-centered classical Christian school opening in Englewood, on Chicago’s South Side, with its first day of school on August 23, 2027. It is a ministry of Urban Youth Outreach, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that has served Englewood families since 1986. Archer opens with kindergarten through 2nd grade, adds one grade each year to become a full K–12 school, and keeps every class to 14 students.

Is there a classical Christian school on the South Side of Chicago?

There is no classical Christian school in Englewood and only a handful anywhere on the South Side. Archer will be the first in the neighborhood—a school families can walk to, in a community where 43% of households do not own a car. Our Why Englewood page lays out the need in numbers.

What is classical Christian education?

A classical education follows the Trivium, matching instruction to each child’s stage: the Grammar School (K–5) fills eager minds with facts, language, and wonder; the Logic School (6–8) trains students to reason, question, and argue well; and the Rhetoric School (9–12) forms graduates who speak and write with wisdom and eloquence. At Archer every subject—Latin, literature, history, mathematics, science, music, art, Scripture, and applied arts such as archery and woodworking—is taught through a biblical lens, so students learn to think clearly, love rightly, and serve faithfully.

How much does Archer cost?

The true cost of an Archer education is about $12,000 per student each year, but tuition is not a posted price. Every family contributes monthly, an amount set individually and confidentially by what the family is able to give—typically about ten percent of the cost—and ministry partners cover the rest. No mission-aligned family is turned away for financial reasons.

Who can apply, and when?

Families of children entering kindergarten, 1st grade, or 2nd grade in Fall 2027 can apply now. You do not need a church background or a profession of faith to apply. Admissions follows four steps: a conversation and tour, an application and family story, a partnership interview, and an enrollment decision.

How can I support education in Englewood through Archer?

Partners make the school possible. Give monthly or one-time at archerchristian.org/partner: a Student Sponsor gift of $10,800 ($900 a month) funds one child’s year, three Fletching Partners at $3,600 each fund one student together, and gifts toward the estimated $400,000 in launch costs open the doors. Gifts are tax-deductible through Urban Youth Outreach (EIN 36-3382527), and donor-advised fund, check, stock, and IRA gifts are welcome. Founding teachers are also wanted.

Where is Archer located?

Archer is based at 672 West Marquette Road in Englewood (Chicago, IL 60621), the home of Urban Youth Outreach near 67th and Union, and will open in the neighborhood it serves—a school families can walk to. Archer is modeled on Hope Academy in Minneapolis, coached through its launch by the Spreading Hope Network, and guided by Clapham School in Wheaton, its local sister school.
