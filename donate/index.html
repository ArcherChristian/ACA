More Ways to Give

Give the Way That Works for You

Many of our partners give through a donor-advised fund, an IRA, appreciated stock, or a multi-year pledge. Whatever the method, every gift is made to Urban Youth Outreach and designated for Archer Christian Academy.

Details You’ll Need

Legal name
Urban Youth Outreach (DBA Archer Christian Academy)
Tax ID / EIN
36-3382527
Mailing address for gifts
1507 E 53rd St, Box 691
Chicago, IL 60615
Designation
“Archer Christian Academy” or “Archer Cornerstone Fund”

Please include the designation on any grant, check, or transfer so your gift reaches Archer. Questions? Email daniel@archerchristian.org or call 312-539-0644.

Donor-Advised Fund

Many Archer partners give through the National Christian Foundation. Log in to your NCF Giving Fund—or Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, or any other sponsor—search for Urban Youth Outreach (EIN 36-3382527), and designate your grant to Archer Christian Academy.

DAF grants often arrive without your name attached. A quick email lets us watch for your gift and thank you properly.

Check by Mail

Make checks payable to Urban Youth Outreach with “Archer Christian Academy” in the memo line, and mail to 1507 E 53rd St, Box 691, Chicago, IL 60615.

Gifts from an IRA

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA through a Qualified Charitable Distribution. The gift counts toward your required minimum distribution and is excluded from your taxable income. Ask your IRA custodian to send the distribution to Urban Youth Outreach using the details above.

Stock & Appreciated Securities

Giving stock you have held for more than a year lets you avoid capital gains tax and deduct the full fair market value—often making it the most tax-efficient way to give. Contact Daniel to arrange a transfer, and let us know the security and share count so we can identify your gift when it arrives.

Multi-Year Pledges

Founding partners often fulfill an Archer, Bow, or Arrow Partner gift over two or three years. A pledge lets you commit now and give on a schedule that works for you. Talk with Daniel about a pledge.

Employer Matching

Many employers match charitable gifts, sometimes doubling your impact. Ask your HR department whether your company has a matching program and submit Urban Youth Outreach (EIN 36-3382527) as the recipient.

Urban Youth Outreach is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. This information is not tax advice—please consult your tax or financial advisor about the best way to give.

Your Gift at Work

Archer Christian Academy is building something rare—a school where rigorous academics, deep character formation, and the Gospel meet in one of Chicago’s most under-resourced communities. Because every family’s tuition is set by what they are able to contribute, generous partners bridge the gap between tuition and the true cost of a world-class education.

Partnership Levels

  • Archer Partner — $25,000: The Archer is the school itself—a foundational gift that helps establish the academy.
  • Bow Partner — $10,000: The Bow is our educational model—equips curriculum, teacher training, and classrooms.
  • Arrow Partner — $5,000: The Arrow is the student—bridges the gap for a child whose family gives what they are able.

The Archer Cornerstone Fund

Every great institution is built on a strong foundation. The Cornerstone Fund is our campaign to lay the groundwork for Archer Christian Academy—not for operations, but for establishment. Gifts serve three mission-critical goals:

  • Secure Our Founding Team. Bring on the key personnel and strategic partners required for a successful launch.
  • Establish Our Home. Secure and prepare our physical location in Englewood.
  • Build Our Plan. Cover the administrative, legal, and strategic consulting costs needed to build an excellent, sustainable school from day one.

A Cornerstone gift is a first-in, foundational investment. You are not just supporting a school—you are one of its builders.
