Teach at Archer

Help launch a school—and shape the classrooms Englewood’s children deserve.

Formation Over Information

Christ-centered, classical teaching that shapes mind, body, and soul—wonder and truth, not test-prep.

Small By Design

14 students per class. You will truly know every child in your room—and be known by them.

Trained & Supported

Classical pedagogy training and coaching through our curriculum partnership with Clapham School. No prior classical experience required—we grow our teachers.

The Position

Elementary Teacher, Full Time

Open positions
Kindergarten, First Grade, Second Grade
School year
2027–2028
Location
Englewood, Chicago, IL 60621
Compensation
Salary is based on education and classroom teaching experience, with benefits including health care.

Position Details

  • Responsible for instruction of students, including planning, preparation, instructional delivery, and assessments, using curriculum provided by Archer Christian Academy.
  • Communicate positively and effectively with students, parents, classroom aides, school administration, and other faculty.
  • Shepherd students in spiritual matters, cultivating healthy spiritual formation.
  • Participate in professional development, school activities, and programs as noted on the staff calendar.

Who We Are Looking For

  • Teachers who love Christ and love children, and embrace our Statement of Faith
  • Educators hungry to master their craft in the classical tradition—we train, you grow
  • People who believe Englewood’s children deserve the best education in the city
  • Builders—founding teachers help shape a school, not just fill a role

General Qualifications

  • Applicant must have an active relationship with God, be regularly engaging with his Word, actively engaged in a local church, and be growing in his or her faith.
  • A love for the children of Chicago and a desire to equip them with an academically rigorous, comprehensive, classical Christian education.
  • Willing to be flexible and be a team player in the midst of a start-up environment.
  • Bachelor’s degree in education and previous teaching experience preferred.
