Founding Partners

Invest in a Truer Future

On August 23, 2027, Lord willing, forty-two kindergartners, first graders, and second graders will take their seats in a school that does not exist yet. Founding partners will bring it into being.

Why This Matters

The Opportunity Before Us

On a Thursday afternoon the doorbell rings, and rings, and rings, as twenty or so children come through the gate of the after-school club where this school was conceived. There we fight for the scraps of the day, working with distracted and tired children who are being failed by a broken system. Archer is our answer: a school that has the whole day, and has it from kindergarten.

Why Englewood. Why Now.

For four decades, Urban Youth Outreach has served families in this community. We know these families, we know this neighborhood, and we know what it takes to build something that lasts.

There is no classical Christian school in Englewood. Archer will be the first, born from everything we have learned, and a school families can walk to in a neighborhood where more than four households in ten do not own a car.

See the need in numbers →

A Proven Network Behind Us

Archer is modeled on Hope Academy, which has served Minneapolis for twenty-five years, and is guided through its launch by the Spreading Hope Network, which grew out of Hope’s work and has helped establish more than twenty classical Christian schools in under-resourced urban communities.

Clapham School in Wheaton partners with us on curriculum and teacher training.

Bridging the Gap

The true cost of an Archer education is about $12,000 per student each year. Families contribute roughly ten percent, as they are able—because cost should never decide who belongs—and founding partners bridge the rest.

Founding Partnerships

An Invitation to Build

Every child in Englewood carries the image of God and the potential for greatness. What they are missing is not ability—it is aim. A founding partner holds a seat open for one of those children, and seat by seat a generation is launched.

It will take $400,000 to open the doors: teachers and other staff; finding and preparing a building; furniture, books, and curriculum; and everything else a school needs before the first bell rings. Our newsletter carries the latest count of what has been committed and what remains.

Founding partnerships run from a Supporter gift of $50 a month to an Archer Partner launch gift of $25,000, with Student Sponsor, Semester Partner, and Fletching Partner levels between them—each given once or spread across the year. The full list, with what each level funds, sits beside the giving form on the Give page.

Built-In Accountability

Archer Christian Academy is governed by the Board of Directors of Urban Youth Outreach, the 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 36-3382527) that receives every gift. Our budget is lean and grounded in real-world data from comparable schools. Network oversight from the Spreading Hope Network and transparent financial reporting ensure every dollar is invested wisely.
